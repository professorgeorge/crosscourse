# Crosscourse: User Manual & Practical Guide

Welcome to **Crosscourse**, an autonomous co-design studio and collaborative workspace for developing shared international courses across partner universities.

Crosscourse is a local-first Progressive Web App (PWA). There are no user accounts, no tracking, and no central servers. All course data stays securely on your device, and you can collaborate seamlessly with international colleagues by exchanging lightweight project files.

## 1. Core Principles

Crosscourse is designed around specific pedagogical and operational commitments:

* **Faculty & Course Developer Sovereignty:** AI proposes, professors decide. No AI generation silently overwrites your curriculum or replaces human judgment.
* **Consortium Equity:** Designed for two or more institutions (up to 9 partner universities). No cohort is treated merely as "informants" or "case material." Every cohort actively investigates, critiques, and co-creates.
* **Interdependence with Resilience:** Collaborative tasks require student contributions from each campus, while built-in fallback contingencies protect cohorts if an international partner experiences unforeseen calendar delays.
* **Sovereign Grading:** Partners agree on shared evaluation criteria (evaluating reasoning, evidence, and cross-cultural engagement rather than English fluency), but each institution translates marks according to its own grading scale.
* **Privacy & Data Ownership:** Projects live in your browser's private local storage. Your private AI API keys never leave your computer and are never included in exported project files.

## 2. Quick Start: Co-Design a Course in 3 Minutes

Follow these four steps on the Crosscourse home screen to generate a complete course:

1. **Add Partner Campuses:** Specify your lead institution (Campus 1) and your international partner universities. Click **+ Add Partner Campus** to include additional universities (up to 9 campuses).
2. **Choose Scope & Pacing:**
   * Select **Shared international module** (embedded in existing courses) or **Co-developed course** (full new course).
   * Pick your duration: 5-week intensive, 7 or 8-week accelerated, 12, 14, or 16-week full semester, or custom weeks.
3. **Select Mobility Format:** Choose 100% Virtual Exchange, Blended with Reciprocal Student Immersion Visit, Faculty Mobility Colloquium, Parallel Synchronized Studios, or Custom Mobility.
4. **Enter Topic or Raw Syllabus Notes:**
   * Type a topic or paste rough syllabus notes in the inquiry box.
   * *Tip:* Click **Convert Rough Notes to Structured Prompt** to format your thoughts into a structured outline with 1 click.
5. **Launch Co-Design:** Click **Autonomously Generate Deliverables** (or **Co-Design with AI Partner Professors**). In seconds, Crosscourse generates your complete 3-Pillar Course Blueprint with all classroom assets ready for download.

## 3. Setting Up a University Consortium (2 to 9 Campuses)

Crosscourse natively supports multilateral partnerships:

* **Lead Campus (Campus 1):** Represents your institution and course context.
* **Partner Campuses (Campus 2 to 9):** Represents each international colleague's institution, country, and course details.
* **Visual Identity:** Each campus is automatically assigned a distinct institutional color dot and banner border across all weekly activity maps and cohort contribution cards.
* **Adding or Removing Campuses:** Click **+ Add Partner Campus** on the launch screen or manage campuses inside **Consortium Campuses** in the project navigation.

## 4. Choosing Duration, Pacing & Cross-Country Mobility

Collaborative international courses require clear synchronization across differing academic calendars:

### Duration Options
* **5-Week Accelerated Intensive:** Best for targeted collaborative modules embedded during the shared overlap window of two different university semesters.
* **7 or 8-Week Accelerated Term:** Ideal for quarter systems, intensive summer institutes, or midterm international segments.
* **12, 14, or 16-Week Full Semester:** For semester-long courses co-developed and taught in lockstep across institutions.
* **Custom Weeks:** Enter any custom week count to match your specific institutional calendar.

### Cross-Country Mobility Formats
* **100% Fully Virtual Exchange:** Students collaborate online using asynchronous shared documents and scheduled live video workshops.
* **Blended Virtual + Student Immersion Visit:** Virtual team formation followed by a 1-week reciprocal student travel and field study immersion visit on the partner campus.
* **Blended Virtual + Faculty Mobility:** Virtual collaborative teaching supplemented by visiting faculty guest colloquia and co-lectures.
* **Dual-Campus Synchronized Labs:** Synchronous parallel lab work or design studios running simultaneously across time zones.
* **Custom Mobility:** Specify custom travel, community field practicums, or hybrid residency models.

## 5. The 3-Pillar Course Blueprint

When your course is generated, it opens in the **3-Pillar Course Blueprint**:

### Pillar 1: Consortium & Vision
* **Partner Context Cards:** Summarizes disciplinary priorities, student levels, time zones, local outcomes, and technological access across all partner campuses.
* **Driving Challenge & Inquiry Question:** The central collaborative problem that neither student cohort could solve alone.
* **Consortium Charter:** Clear rules of engagement covering decision rights, response time commitments, language accommodations, and intellectual property attribution.

### Pillar 2: Weekly Arc & Activities
* **Milestone Arc:** A structured week-by-week sequence following the six pedagogical stages: Establish Trust -> Investigate Locally -> Compare -> Critique -> Co-Create -> Reflect.
* **Campus Cohort Roles:** Explicit cards outlining what students from each participating university contribute to every activity.
* **Student Directions & Contingency Fallbacks:** Clear instructions for students, with documented alternatives if international partners face connectivity or schedule delays.

### Pillar 3: Classroom Deliverables
Your centralized download hub containing five ready-to-use packages:
1. **LMS Course Cartridge (.imscc & .zip):** Ready for 1-click import into Canvas, Blackboard, Moodle, or Brightspace.
2. **Approved Course Syllabus (HTML & Print to PDF):** Complete institutional document with alignment matrix and fillable signature blocks.
3. **Student Activity Guide & Handouts (Markdown):** Ready to copy or upload into your classroom LMS.
4. **Consortium Partnership Charter (HTML):** Formal inter-institutional agreement.
5. **Synchronized Calendar (.ics):** Importable into Google Calendar, Microsoft Outlook, and Apple Calendar.
6. **Portable Project Archive (.json):** Self-contained course backup file.

## 6. Exporting to Your LMS (Canvas, Blackboard, Moodle, Brightspace)

Crosscourse generates native **IMS Common Cartridges (.imscc)** for rapid LMS setup:

### How to Import into Canvas:
1. In Crosscourse, navigate to **Pillar 3: Classroom Deliverables** and click **Export Cartridge (.IMSCC)**.
2. Open your Canvas course and click **Settings** in the left-hand course menu.
3. Click **Import Course Content** on the right side.
4. Under *Content Type*, select **Common Cartridge 1.x Package**.
5. Choose the downloaded `.imscc` file, select **All Content**, and click **Import**.
6. Canvas will automatically create your weekly module arc, assignment pages, student directions, and SpeedGrader rubrics.

### How to Import into Blackboard:
1. In Blackboard Learn, navigate to **Course Management** -> **Packages and Utilities** -> **Import Package / View Logs**.
2. Click **Import Package**, select the downloaded `.imscc` file, check the appropriate content areas, and click **Submit**.

### How to Import into Moodle:
1. In Moodle, go to your course and select **Course Management** (or Gear icon) -> **Restore**.
2. Upload the `.imscc` file into the backup area and click **Restore**. Follow the on-screen steps to merge the modules into your course.

## 7. AI Options: Local WebGPU, Ollama, Cloud, or Offline Templates

Crosscourse gives you complete freedom over how AI is used:

### Option A: In-Browser Local AI (WebGPU)
* **Supported Browsers:** Microsoft Edge, Google Chrome, Mozilla Firefox (130+), Safari 18+, Brave, Opera.
* **How it works:** Uses WebGPU hardware acceleration to download compact open-weights models (`SmolLM2-360M`, `Qwen2.5-0.5B`, `Llama-3.2-1B`) directly into your browser tab cache.
* **Benefits:** 100% free, runs on-device, no servers, zero API keys, and works offline once cached.

### Option B: Local AI on your Computer (Ollama / LM Studio)
* **Supported Browsers:** Any browser on Windows, Mac, or Linux.
* **How it works:** Communicates with models running locally on your computer via [Ollama](https://ollama.com) (`http://localhost:11434/v1`) or [LM Studio](https://lmstudio.ai) (`http://localhost:1234/v1`).
* **Benefits:** Use your own local workstation models (`llama3.2`, `mistral`, `qwen2.5`) with complete privacy.

### Option C: Free Google Gemini Cloud Tier
* **How it works:** Uses Google's generative language API. You can generate a free API key at [Google AI Studio](https://aistudio.google.com/app/apikey) with no credit card required.
* **Benefits:** Fast execution and nuanced pedagogical reasoning.

### Option D: Deterministic Pedagogical Templates (No AI Needed)
* **How it works:** Crosscourse has built-in deterministic pedagogical templates for all co-design stages.
* **Benefits:** Zero setup, zero keys, zero downloads, and completely functional offline.

## 8. Decentralized Collaboration (Share & Sync)

Crosscourse operates without a centralized database. Teaching partners collaborate peer-to-peer:

1. **Send an Update:** In the project navigation, click **Share & sync**. Click **Download file & draft email** (or **Download file only**) to export a `.crosscourse.json` update packet.
2. **Share with Partners:** Attach the `.crosscourse.json` file to an email or save it in a shared drive (Google Drive, OneDrive, Microsoft Teams).
3. **Open a Partner's Update:** When your international colleague receives the file, they open Crosscourse in their browser and click **Open update file** (or click **Import Course Backup** on the home screen).
4. **Smart Item-by-Item Merge:** Crosscourse automatically reconciles the updates:
   * New activities and outcomes are merged in.
   * Mutual approvals and votes are combined.
   * If two colleagues edit the same item concurrently, the newer version is kept and the alternative is safely stored in **Concurrent Edits** so no work is ever lost.

## 9. Backups, Restores & Project Portability

You can save and restore your work at any time:

* **Export a Single Course Backup:** In Pillar 3, click **Export Project (.JSON)** to download `<course-title>-consortium-course.json`.
* **Export All Courses on This Device:** Open **Settings** from the bottom-left menu and click **Export all projects** to download a complete archive of every project on your machine.
* **Importing Backups:** On the home screen, click **Import Course Backup (.json)** and select your file. The full course structure will restore immediately.

## 10. Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Do my international partners need to install software or create accounts?**  
A: No. Crosscourse runs directly in any modern web browser. Partners simply open the link, load the shared `.crosscourse.json` project file, and begin collaborating.

**Q: Can I co-design courses with 3 or more universities?**  
A: Yes. Crosscourse is engineered for multi-campus consortia of 2 to 9 universities. You can add partner institutions dynamically, and activities support designated contributions for every campus.

**Q: What if our academic calendars only overlap for a few weeks?**  
A: Crosscourse calculates the exact overlap window between partner calendars. For shorter overlaps, choose the 5-week accelerated module format, and the weekly arc will calibrate to fit within your shared teaching window.

**Q: Does Crosscourse send my syllabus data to external servers?**  
A: Never. All calculations, syllabus parsing, calendar overlap checks, and local file storage happen entirely inside your browser. If you use In-Browser WebGPU or offline templates, no data leaves your device. If you use a cloud API key, requests go directly from your browser to your chosen provider with your private key.

**Q: Can I install Crosscourse as a desktop application?**  
A: Yes. Crosscourse is a Progressive Web App (PWA). In Chrome, Edge, or Safari, click the Install icon in the address bar (or go to Settings -> Install Crosscourse) to install it as a standalone desktop app that works offline.
